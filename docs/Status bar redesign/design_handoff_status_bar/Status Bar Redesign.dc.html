Status bar redesign — two directions

Both are built for one-handed use in bright sun and keep the map clear. Block name, variety and the zone you're standing in are the hero readout; GPS Walking is treated as the primary action, not a buried button. Compare 1A — Field Dock and 1B — Glance HUD.

1A

Field Dock

A thumb-anchored bottom sheet. Collapsed, it shows only the hero readout and one big GPS button — the map owns the screen. Drag up for jump-to-block, zone filters and follow controls.

Handoff spec · iOS + Android
Safe areas — pad the sheet with padding-bottom: max(22px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom)) so it clears the iPhone home indicator and Android gesture bar. Add viewport-fit=cover to the viewport meta tag to enable the inset values.
Tap targets — all controls are ≥44 px, meeting Apple HIG and Material minimums for gloved, one-handed field use.
Rendering — standard flex / radius / shadow / gradient CSS only — identical in iOS Safari and Android Chrome (the PWA runtime).
1B

Glance HUD

Splits status from action. A slim top chip carries the glanceable readout; a big round GPS button lives in the natural thumb corner. Tapping the chip drops a controls card — the rest of the time the map is almost fully visible.
